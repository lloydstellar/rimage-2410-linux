New job

1. Label

A square image works best: {{ canvas }} x {{ canvas }} pixels is exactly 120 mm at 400 dpi. The printed area is a ring; its size depends on the type of disc (below). Download a template with those sizes: Photoshop (PSD, with layers) or PNG.

    The print extends half a millimetre past the white coating, so no white edge stays visible. For a new type of disc, measure where the white starts and ends, then choose Custom size.

    2. Burn

    Data CD: one ISO file. Audio CD: choose one or more WAV, FLAC or MP3 files, then put the CD together (order, titles, pauses). Or a BIN/CUE pair. There is no such thing as an "ISO of an audio CD". Leave empty to print only.

      Tracks

      Set the order with the arrows. The pause is silence before a track; the first track always starts after 2 seconds. A 24-bit master is converted to 16 bit with dither when burning, which is the audio CD format.

        Disc

        Quality, speed and codes

        TPDF adds a very quiet, even noise and prevents distortion in quiet passages and fading tails. Noise-shaped dither moves that noise to high frequencies where the ear is less sensitive, but the total noise is higher.

        Only needed if a track gets a warning: a peak above 0 dBFS or a true peak above 0 dBTP. A master with a ceiling of, for example, -0.1 dBTP can stay as it is.

        Only fill in UPC and ISRC with real codes, for example from your distributor. They make no difference for playback.

        3. Job

        Off: burn only, for example on discs that are already printed. The artwork is kept for a release.

        Release

        Save everything (artwork, audio, track list and settings) as a release to make the CD again later.
